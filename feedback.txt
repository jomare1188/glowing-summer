[2:21 p. m., 28/9/2026] Tatiana USA: este es el problema mira The −0.16 means d20 still has more heterozygotes than expected. There are three possible reasons, and the two checks tell them apart.

1. Paralogs the mask missed. This is the most likely. Two copies of a gene get collapsed onto one place in the C. americana reference, so every tree looks heterozygous there. The first check detects this: if many SNPs still show all 9 trees heterozygous, paralogs remain and the mask needs to be stricter.

2. A problem with one or two samples, for example contamination or DNA mixed from two plants. A contaminated sample looks heterozygous almost everywhere. The second check detects this: that tree's Ho would stand far above the others. The fix is to remove or re-examine that sample, not to change the mask.

3. Real biology. In a very small population with few parents, a mild excess of heterozygotes is expected, and seedlings from the same mother (like the PV trees) add a bit more. If the two checks come out clean, meaning no spike of all-heterozygous SNPs and no outlier tree, then part of the −0.16 is probably real. You can report it as a result instead of treating it as an error.

The reason −0.06 is the reference point: with only 9 trees, Fis comes out slightly negative even when nothing is wrong, just from small sample size. Anything well below that needs an explanation, and these checks give one.

[30 sep 2026 - Jorge] PUNTO 2 - FILTROS DE GENOTIPO ANTES DE LOS ALL-SITES ENMASCARADOS
Por que: aun con la mascara v2 quedan heterocigotos falsos. En un heterocigoto real cerca
de la mitad de las lecturas lleva cada alelo; segun la profundidad de cada llamado, solo
2-4% de los heterocigotos reales deberian tener fraccion alterna < 0.25 o > 0.75. En d20
enmascarado era 33-58% en seis arboles. Eso infla la heterocigosidad individual y corta
ROH reales.
Filtros elegidos (iguales en variantes e invariantes; se usa FORMAT/DP, no GQ, porque los
invariantes traen RGQ):
  - DP < 3 -> genotipo faltante
  - heterocigoto que falla la prueba binomial de balance (p < 0.01) -> faltante
  - despues, solo sitios con >= 80% de genotipos llamados
